Add folder

Pick a folder. It becomes an album with every photo in it and in its subfolders. Your files are not copied or moved.

Albums

An album is a folder. Removing one only takes it off this list: no photo or folder is deleted. The upload album is where uploads and drops land; it opens with the PIN. Type a new name and press Enter to rename; the arrows change the order.

About

Negative: a self-hosted photo server that works with the Immich phone app. Your folders stay where they are; a picked folder is an album.
